Method

  1. Source. NASA FIRMS near-real-time detections from three VIIRS instruments (Suomi NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21), 375 m resolution.
  2. Filter. Night passes only, inside 3.8 to 7.0 N and 4.5 to 9.0 E. Flares burn all night; farm fires mostly burn by day.
  3. Record. Each complete night becomes a file of the untouched detections. Its SHA-256 goes into a Solana program that can write a night once and never again.
  4. Classify. Detections within 1 km form a site. A site seen on 3 or more nights within 7 days is a persistent flare.
  5. Honest numbers. FRP is the heat the sensor sees, in megawatts. It is not turned into gas volume or CO2, because that needs a calibration this project does not claim.

The full method is METHOD.md. Its hash is fixed in the registry account, so the rules can't change without a new registry.
